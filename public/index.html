How to play
  • Goal: fill two whole rows with stone. Each full row locks into the wall as a course. Two courses hang the Fish Gate doors and win the round.
  • Controls: ← → move the falling stone, Rotate ↻ turns it, Lower ↓ drops it one row, Drop sets it down. Keys: arrows, ↑ to rotate, Space to drop.
  • Your Watchman is your teammate (your agent). They see raids coming but not your grid. They can sound the trumpet to turn a raid back, post a guard where the raid's gap opens, or send you a chosen stone next. Talk to them.
  • Raids: a raid that lands pushes a row of rubble up from the bottom. The round is lost if stones or rubble reach the top.
